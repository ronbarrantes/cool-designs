## How to use this list

This is a finding queue, not a ranking. For each site we eventually want to capture:

- the first useful entry point;
- one complete workflow, not just a landing page;
- the primary user and job to be done;
- happy path and meaningful exception states;
- data, role, approval, and payment handoffs;
- onboarding and activation mechanics;
- visual hierarchy, density, motion, and copy patterns;
- what is worth recreating as practice;
- what is too specific, legally sensitive, or not appropriate to copy.

The next pass should sample ten sites at a time, preserve raw observations, and only then produce distilled patterns or recreations.
