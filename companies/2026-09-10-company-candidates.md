## Next pass

For each candidate, capture the best public screens and flows, then distill:

- entry point and user intent;
- happy path;
- important empty, loading, error, and exception states;
- handoffs between people, systems, or roles;
- trust and conversion mechanics;
- notable visual patterns;
- what could be recreated safely as inspiration;
- what should not be copied or treated as original work.
