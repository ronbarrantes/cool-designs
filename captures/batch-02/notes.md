## Duolingo

- **Source:** https://www.duolingo.com/
- **Capture:** `batch-02/duolingo-home.png`
- **Observed:** A sparse landing screen centers a friendly product illustration beside a short value statement, with a bright primary `Get started` action and a secondary account action. A persistent language strip at the bottom exposes English, Chess, Math, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Portuguese choices.
- **Workflow pattern:** choose entry intent → start or resume account path → choose a learning surface.
- **Notable state language:** the primary and returning-user actions are separated clearly, while the language strip remains available without competing with the main action.
- **Study target:** how a playful brand keeps the first decision small and gives alternate learning categories a persistent but quiet navigation rail.

## Shopify

- **Source:** https://www.shopify.com/
- **Capture:** `batch-02/shopify-home.png`
- **Observed:** A fixed-looking top navigation overlays a full-bleed editorial video hero. The header keeps product navigation, pricing, enterprise, login, and a prominent `Start for free` action visible. The hero pairs a large short headline with supporting copy and a matching primary action, while the next section is already visible at the bottom of the viewport.
- **Workflow pattern:** brand promise → immediate signup entry → broad commerce capability framing → deeper product paths.
- **Notable state language:** the repeated `Start for free` action appears in both navigation and hero, reducing the distance from interest to signup.
- **Study target:** how high-impact media and restrained navigation establish a strong first-viewport hierarchy while preserving a clear conversion path.
